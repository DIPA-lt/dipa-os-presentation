03 · The shift

The work writes the record. Friday is not a rebuild.

Without it

The sale is scattered across tools.

1Research lives in tabs and a spreadsheet.
2The call is on a phone. The notes are somewhere else.
3A meeting recording stays in a folder. Tasks are typed later, if anyone remembers.
4Monday receives leads that have no date.
5The manager asks for the week. Someone rebuilds it by hand.
6A question about method or a past call means searching chat history.

With DIPA OS

Each step leaves something the next step uses.

1Search, score and the Monday check happen before a dial.
2The call, the recording and the next step stay on the lead.
3A session becomes a transcript, tasks, and a page in Knowledge.
4Only a warm lead goes to Monday, and it stays in sync.
5Plan versus fact is on Sales OS the same day.
6Copilot answers from the record, the CRM and the knowledge base.

04 · The sales path

Five steps. Each one leaves something behind.

→
Step 1

Find

Pick a segment. Score the company. Check Monday before research time is spent.

  • Industry, size, regionMetal fabrication, 50–200 people, Lithuania
  • A / B / C / D fitSame rubric for every seller. C and D stop.
  • Already owned?Monday is checked before research time is spent.
Leaves a company worth a call
→
Step 2

Prepare

One screen before the dial: the person, why this company, and the opening.

  • Direct number, or switchboardA switchboard is labelled. It is not presented as a direct line.
  • Why nowThe course builds that match this company’s work.
  • Three opening questionsWritten for this company, not a generic script.
Leaves a prepared seller
→
Step 3

Call

Click to call from the lead. Recording, transcript and a coach sit beside it.

  • ConsentIf the person does not agree, the call ends and the transcript is deleted.
  • Live promptsThe coach sits beside the conversation.
  • Audio on the leadThe recording is not a file in someone’s downloads.
Leaves a recorded conversation
→
Step 4

Review

The seller confirms the summary, the temperature and the next date.

  • Summary the seller acceptsNothing is written until they confirm it.
  • Task with an ownerFollow-up, due date, and the reason it exists.
  • CalendarA meeting is created when a time was agreed.
Leaves a next step with a date
Step 5

Decide

Warm goes to Monday. Everyone else stays in DIPA OS for a follow-up.

  • Right companyFits the offer. Someone owns the problem.
  • A next stepA visit, a proposal, or a named follow-up.
  • A dateWithout a date it stays in DIPA OS.
Leaves a clean pipeline

09 · Sessions

A meeting becomes tasks, and a page the team can search.

1 · Capture

Record in the app

From the header, on any page. Pick who was in the room and what kind of session it was.

  • Browser tabZoom, Meet or Teams, with the meeting audio.
  • One app windowThe Zoom client, or another single app.
  • The whole screenWhatever you switch to during the session.
  • MicrophoneAn in-person meeting in the room.
2 · Understand

Transcript and summary

When the recording finishes, an agent reads it and files it by month.

  • Full transcriptSearchable by title, people and what was said.
  • A short summaryWritten for someone who was not in the room.
  • CommitmentsWho said they would do what.
  • VideoStays attached when the capture had a picture.
3 · Act

Action items become tasks

Each item carries the reason, a definition of done, and the line it came from.

  • OwnerMatched to a team member when the name is clear.
  • Due dateTaken from what was said, anchored to the meeting day.
  • Accepted firstA task is created only after someone confirms it.
4 · Keep

Filed into Knowledge

The transcript is stored so Copilot and Assistants can retrieve it later.

  • Separate from the playbooksTranscripts have their own knowledge base.
  • Grouped by quarterThe library stays browsable as the months add up.
  • Ask this sessionA chat on the recording, grounded in the transcript.
Strategic meeting Client call Internal sync Training Interview Other
A sales call recorded from the lead follows the same path: audio, transcript, confirmed summary, task. Sessions covers the meetings that are not a single outbound call.

12 · What changes

Sellers perform. Managers see it without asking twice.

For the seller

  • No time on a company another seller already owns.
  • A direct number, or a clear label when only the switchboard exists.
  • The first minute of the call is written for that company.
  • After the call, the task and the meeting are created from the confirmation.
  • A recorded meeting turns into tasks with an owner and a quote.
  • Monday is one button, used when the lead has a date.
  • Copilot answers from the lead, the transcript and the playbook.

For the manager

  • Plan against fact for calls, meetings, qualified leads and revenue.
  • The funnel shows the step where the week stopped.
  • Each seller’s row is separate from the company total.
  • Call quality is a score from the conversation, and it can be disputed.
  • Workshop fill sits next to the pipeline.
  • Session recordings are searchable, not trapped in a folder.
  • Ask Copilot which number is behind, and why.

What a partner can rely on

  • A lead from your form or sheet is created in DIPA OS.
  • It is screened before a seller spends an hour on it.
  • A warm conversation becomes a Monday lead with the summary attached.
  • The weekly board shows whether that work became meetings.
  • A meeting about your lead can be recorded, tasked and found again.
