05

Virtualus operatorius

Virtualaus operatoriaus juosta apima keturis darbus. Jie visi grąžina rezultatą į tą pačią tiesą apie lead'ą.

Atsiliepia į skambučius

Pasisveikina, per DIPA OS atpažįsta skambinantįjį pagal numerį ir gauna įmonės ar lead'o vardą bei priskirtą pardavėją.

Nukreipia arba rezervuoja

Žinomą skambinantįjį nukreipia į priskirtą pardavėją, rodydamas vardą; nežinomą – užfiksuoja ketinimą ir rezervuoja laiką kaip naują užklausą.

Šildo CD banką

Skambina šaltiems CD banko lead'ams pagal scenarijų, kvalifikuoja ir, susitarus, perduoda pardavėjui priimti (SAL).

Įrašas ir vertinimas

Kiekvienas skambutis įrašomas į Drive; DIPA OS jį importuoja, transkribuoja, įvertina (Call QA) ir užrašo veiklą lead'o kortelėje.

06

Ką piltuvėlis įtvirtina

Taisyklės

  • Vienas kelias: kiekvienas įrašas visada yra viename etape.
  • Kiekvienas skambutis (inbound, CD šildymas, discovery) eina tą patį įrašo → transkripcijos → Call QA kelią.
  • Išeiti iš etapo galima tik tada, kai tenkinama sąlyga; ją tikrina sistema.
  • Pralaimėjimo kelias prieinamas iš priėmimo, kvalifikacijos ir sprendimo; priežastis privaloma.
  • Parkuotas įrašas grįžta į Sandėlį pats, reaktyvavimo dieną.
  • Piltuvėlis nesibaigia ties Won: papildomas pardavimas grįžta į pradžią kaip šiltas lead'as.

Kas sprendžia

  • Virtualus operatorius atsiliepia, atpažįsta, nukreipia arba rezervuoja ir šildo CD banką; įsipareigojimų klientui neduoda.
  • DI juostoje – tik juodraščiai ir pasiūlymai («A1», «A2»). DI nepriima, nesiunčia ir nesuteikia nuolaidų.
  • Pardavėjas priima lead'ą, patvirtina laukus ir siunčia pasiūlymą.
  • Sales Lead tvirtina kainą žemiau grindų ir kas savaitę iš priežasčių išrenka vieną apribojimą.
  • Sistema («A3») vykdo vartus, Won blokavimą, pinigų susiejimą ir GP skaičiavimą.

DIPA · 27 October 2026 · Process model

DIPA OS processes: UML activity diagrams

Four diagrams show the ideal model. The funnel is one record's path. Signals are how the system compares a fact with a rule and who it tells. The day is how those signals become tasks. The week is the snapshot, the one constraint and coaching. The conditions come from doc 37 and doc 40, not from today's code.

01

Funnel

Columns are responsibility lanes (UML partitions): client, virtual operator, system, AI, seller, finance, delivery and Sales Lead. Arrows are the control flow. A diamond is a decision with its guard in brackets. The black bar forks Won into cash and delivery, which run in parallel.

Evaluation and signals

The system reads the funnel's facts continuously. A signal is raised only past the line. A safe repair is tried first. What remains becomes an exception with an owner, an action and a deadline. The seller gets their own breach, a repeat goes to Monday, and the owner sees only the five signals past the delegated line.

The day

In the morning the system collects the open signals and builds one list from them. The list fits the seller's free hours; the Sales Lead decides the overflow. In the evening every task is done or has a reason from the closed list. The reasons go to Monday.

The week

On Monday the system stores the forecast snapshot and groups call scores by behaviour. A repeated behaviour becomes one coaching focus, not a task per call. The reasons become one proposed constraint, which the Sales Lead picks. Full capacity pauses new flow. The chosen constraint becomes the next day's rule.

05

Virtual operator

The virtual operator lane covers four jobs. All of them feed the result back into the one truth about the lead.

Answers calls

Greets the caller, identifies them in DIPA OS by number, and gets the company or lead name and the assigned rep.

Routes or books

Routes a known caller to the assigned rep with the name shown; for an unknown caller it captures the intent and books a slot as a new enquiry.

Warms the CD bank

Calls cold CD-bank leads by script, qualifies them and, when booked, hands them to the seller to accept (SAL).

Recording and evaluation

Every call is recorded to Drive; DIPA OS imports it, transcribes it, scores it (Call QA) and writes the activity on the lead's card.

06

What the funnel enforces

Rules

  • One path: every record is always in exactly one stage.
  • Every call (inbound, CD warming, discovery) follows the same recording → transcription → Call QA path.
  • A record leaves a stage only when the guard holds; the system checks it.
  • The loss path opens from acceptance, qualification and decision; a reason is required.
  • A parked record returns to Sandėlis by itself on its reactivation date.
  • The funnel continues after Won: an upsell returns to the start as a warm lead.

Who decides

  • The virtual operator answers, identifies, routes or books, and warms the CD bank; it makes no commitments to the client.
  • The AI lane holds only drafts and suggestions («A1», «A2»). AI does not accept, send or discount.
  • The seller accepts the lead, confirms the fields and sends the proposal.
  • The Sales Lead approves a price below the floor and each week picks one constraint from the reasons.
  • The system («A3») runs the gate, the Won block, cash linking and the GP calculation.
